# Booking, proctoring and the rules of the day

**Level:** Foundations · **Module 11:** Exam readiness 1 · **Page 2 of 4**
**Exams:** all

**After this page you can** book an exam, prepare for online proctoring or a test centre, request an accommodation in the
right order, plan around retakes, and keep a credential current through renewal.

Checked against the Associate, Developer, Architect Foundations and Architect Professional exam guides (version 1.0,
effective July 2026; the registration, policy and renewal sections are the same in all four), the Anthropic Certification
Terms and Conditions, the Anthropic Certification Exam Policy (last updated 25 June 2026), the Pearson VUE pages for
Anthropic exams and for online proctoring, and the Anthropic announcement of the programme, all read on 2026-10-02.
**Fees, rules and deadlines change; read the current guide, the Terms, the Exam Policy and your booking confirmation
before you rely on anything here.**

## Why it matters

A candidate can know the material and still lose the fee: a name that does not match the ID, a booking changed too late,
a second monitor on the desk, an accommodation requested after scheduling. These are rules, not judgment calls, and each is
written down. Reading them once costs less than one forfeited fee.

## The idea

### The booking path

1. **Check eligibility and pick the exam** (page 1). Exam access is through the Claude Partner Network and the Anthropic
   Partner Academy.
2. **Read the three documents before you pay**: the exam guide, the Certification Terms and Conditions and the
   Certification Exam Policy. The guide tells you to read them before registering.
3. **Register and pay.** The fee shown at checkout reflects any discount for your partner tier. Fees on 2026-10-02:
   Associate 99, Developer and Architect Foundations 125, Architect Professional 175 (USD).
4. **Create your Pearson VUE account** from the confirmation instructions and sign in to schedule.
5. **Choose a date and a delivery mode:** online proctoring or a Pearson test centre.
6. **Cancel or reschedule on time.** The exam guides say you may cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the
   appointment, and that changes within 24 hours forfeit the fee. Pearson's page for Anthropic exams gives 48 hours for
   test-centre appointments. The two figures differ, so the safe habit is to **use the longer notice and read your
   confirmation email**, which governs your booking.
7. **Do not no-show, and do not arrive late.** A candidate who does not appear, or who arrives after the permitted
   late-arrival window, forfeits the fee and must register again.

### Your name and your ID

The name on your registration must match your ID exactly. On exam day you must show a valid, unexpired, government-issued
photo ID; if the name differs, you may be refused entry and lose the fee. If a name needs correcting, do it **before**
you schedule, through the certifications support contact given in the guide. Impersonation, including using an ID
that is not yours, is misconduct.

### Online proctoring or a test centre

Both modes follow the same rules of conduct. They differ in logistics.

| | Online proctored | Pearson test centre |
|---|---|---|
| Where | A space you provide | A centre, supervised on site |
| Before | Run Pearson's system test on the computer and connection; find a private, distraction-free space | Travel there; arrive in time for the late-arrival window |
| Monitoring | You consent to monitoring by human proctors and assistive AI tools for the whole test, and must stay within view of the proctor and webcam | Supervised by the centre |
| Network | Pearson's page lists the domains your network must allow for the online test, so check any firewall in advance | Not your concern |
| Change of plan | Reschedule within the notice period | Reschedule within the notice period |

Choose by risk. If your home or office cannot give a quiet room, one monitor, a reliable connection and no interruptions,
a test centre removes four ways to fail for non-knowledge reasons.

### The rules of the day

During the exam you **must**: stay in view of the proctor and webcam if online; keep your workspace clear of notes, books,
phones, secondary monitors and other materials; not talk to anyone; and not capture, copy or reproduce any exam content.
The Exam Policy adds that prohibited behaviour includes **using AI products or services to help you during the exam**,
having more than one monitor, possessing electronic devices or notes (including during breaks), taking unscheduled breaks
unless approved in advance, and misrepresenting your country of residence or where the exam is delivered. Permitted items,
such as scratch paper, are set by Pearson VUE.

**Consequences.** Anthropic may suspend or ban you, require a retake, invalidate your result, not issue or revoke the
credential, and keep the fee. You can appeal a final decision within 14 calendar days of notice, through the programme
(first through the third-party provider where one runs it). The standard-setting outcome and the content of individual items
are not open to appeal.

**Confidentiality.** Before the exam begins you accept a non-disclosure agreement covering all questions, options and
scenarios. If you decline, the session ends and no refund is given. Afterwards you may not describe the questions. You
can describe your preparation, and the course's own questions are written fresh for that reason.

### Accommodations: the order matters

Reasonable accommodations are available for documented disabilities or needs, in line with applicable law. The sequence
is fixed: **request the accommodation through Pearson VUE, wait for approval, and only then schedule the exam.** The
guide says to not schedule your appointment until the request is approved. Both the Exam Policy and the guide say the
request must be submitted and approved before scheduling. Extra time or a break you did not arrange in advance is not
available on the day, and an unapproved break counts as misconduct.

### If you do not pass

Retake waiting periods grow with each failed attempt: **14 days after the first, 30 days after the second, 90 days after
the third**, with at most **four attempts in a rolling 12 months per exam**. Each attempt costs the full fee. Not passing one
exam does not block another. Use the percent-correct by domain on the score report to aim your revision: it tells you where
to study, though it does not decide the result. Retake only when you have changed something about how you prepare; the
mock exam and review bank in this module are for that.

### The credential: validity, renewal and the badge

- **Validity.** 12 months from the date awarded.
- **Renewing on time.** Before it expires you review what has changed and complete a **free, non-proctored assessment** on
  the Anthropic Partner Academy; there is no fee. If the exam content changes significantly, Anthropic may instead require
  holders to retake the full exam.
- **If it lapses.** You must retake the full exam at the full fee. The Exam Policy states that once a certification has
  expired you can no longer renew it and must re-earn it.
- **The badge.** Anthropic's announcement says digital badges are issued through Credly by Pearson. A credential is a
  statement of the period it covers: under the Terms you must not present yourself as currently certified after it
  expires, and you should list the certification period. The Terms also say a certification is not a warranty of your
  abilities, so do not describe it as one.
- **Partners and revocation.** Certification belongs to the individual, not to the partner organisation. Anthropic may
  revoke a certification for breach of the Terms, misconduct or other reasons the Terms list, such as a risk to the
  programme's integrity.

## Traps

1. **Scheduling first, asking for an accommodation later.** The approval must come before the booking, and it cannot be
   arranged on the day.
2. **Trusting one notice period.** The guide and the test-centre page give different cancellation notices; use the longer and
   check your confirmation.
3. **Treating an expired credential as renewable.** Renewal is before expiry and by the free assessment; after a lapse the
   full exam is needed.
